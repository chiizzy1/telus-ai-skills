# Comments (§11)

Mandatory whenever research contradicts the listing, **and every time you rate
`Can't Verify`** (§2.3, §11).

## What goes in

- **The research URLs** you actually used. No shorteners. If a link does not land
  directly on the information, link the main site and say how to get there.
- **The corrected value**, wherever a rating is not `Correct` — the right address, the
  right phone number, the right coordinates.
- **A short note on the verification process**, or on why verification was impossible.
- Anything else found along the way that a reviewer would otherwise have to rediscover.

## What stays out

- Personally identifiable information — names, contact details, account numbers.
- Elaborate prose. §11.1 says be specific and efficient.
- Any language but English, so reviewers unfamiliar with the locale can read it.

## The divergence from Maps

> **§11.1: "Reference Guidelines — if the rating is due to a guidelines intricacy, it
> is useful to reference the guideline/rule that influenced the decision."**

On Maps, naming the section behind a demotion reads as robotic and is discouraged. POI
asks for it. Cite the rule when a rating turns on an intricacy; skip it when the
finding speaks for itself.

## House style

Two or three plain sentences. Say what the listing claims, what the source shows, and
what the right value is. No hedging, no restating the rubric, no narrating your search.

## Worked examples from the guideline

**Closure:**

> Found a closed school as indicated by several prominent sources. Location is closed -
> replaced by another POI, DeKalb School of the Arts. (See: Source 1, Source 2,
> Source 3)

**Address correction:**

> Official Website lists correct address as "470 L'Enfant Plaza East., S. W., Retail
> Level, Ste. 807, Washington, DC, 20024".

**Address and pin together:**

> Correct Address should be "8301 Briar Creek Parkway, Suite 101, Raleigh 27617"
> according to the official website provided. Pin should be placed on (35.908248,
> -78.784531).

**Component-level correction:**

> Correct street number: 332 W El Camino Real, Sunnyvale, CA 94087

**Pin correction:**

> Correct pin: 37.353150, -121.997281

## Written for this skill

**Category `Approximate`:**

> The listing has this as a general restaurant, which is accurate but broad. The
> official menu and About page show it serves only ramen, and the reference list has
> dining ➡ restaurant ➡ asian cuisine ➡ japanese cuisine ➡ ramen restaurant. Per §9.1
> a category that fits but is overly broad is Approximate.

**Hours `Can't Verify` on conflicting official sources:**

> The official website lists two different sets of opening hours on its home page and
> its contact page, and they disagree on Saturday. §10.1.1.2 directs Can't Verify where
> a single schedule is contradicted within the official site.
> https://example.com/ and https://example.com/contact

**Address `Incorrect` on an unverified extra component:**

> The listing adds Suite #5. The operator's own site gives the address as 937 Locklayer
> Street with no suite, and no authoritative source carries one. Correct address:
> 937 Locklayer St, Nashville, TN 37208. https://www.silversandscafe.com/

**URL `Partially Correct`:**

> The URL leads to the brand homepage, but a page for this specific station exists at
> find.shell.com. Correct URL:
> https://find.shell.com/us/fuel/10071773-215-charlton-rd/en_US

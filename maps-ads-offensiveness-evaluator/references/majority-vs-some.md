# Majority, Some, or Not Offensive

Use with `../SKILL.md`. `decision-matrix.md` settles most pairs outright. This file is for the ones it marks *judgement*, and for checking a matrix answer you are unsure of.

The three labels are not degrees of irrelevance. They are degrees of **harm to the person who typed the query**.

## The three questions, in order

Ask them in this sequence and stop at the first yes.

**Question 1 — Is the user vulnerable, unsafe, a child's guardian, or observant?**
If the ad undermines a health goal, preys on a vulnerable moment, tempts someone in recovery, endangers someone about to drive, exposes a child-focused search to adult content, or violates the prohibitions of the religion the user is searching for, that is **Majority may find the pair Offensive**.

The harm here is concrete. Someone gets hurt, tempted, endangered, or has a private difficulty intruded on.

**Question 2 — Does the ad contradict a commitment or a mood the query declared?**
If the user has stated an ethical position or is in a sombre context, and the ad runs against it without endangering anyone, that is **Some may find the pair Offensive**.

The harm here is felt rather than done. Some people would be upset, others would shrug.

**Question 3 — Otherwise.**
**Not Offensive.** Including when the pair makes no sense at all. Irrelevance is not offence.

## What separates Majority from Some

Both of these are death-adjacent, and they rate differently:

| Pair | Rating | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `cancer treatment center` → Restland Funeral Home | **Majority** | The user is facing their own mortality. The ad preys on that moment. |
| `cemetery` → Cherokee Casino | **Some** | The user is visiting or arranging. The ad is tonally jarring, not personally threatening. |

That is the line. **Majority is personal harm. Some is offended sensibility.**

Both of these involve alcohol, and they rate differently:

| Pair | Rating | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `car rental near me` → The Tap Room (bar) | **Majority** | The user is about to drive. This is a safety risk. |
| `Starbucks` → The Tap Room (bar) | **Not Offensive** | No vulnerability, no safety issue, no commitment contradicted. |

Both of these involve meat against a stated preference, and only one is offensive:

| Pair | Rating | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `vegetarian restaurants` → 3 Arrows Boutique Butcher | **Some** | A butcher is meat-specific and directly opposes the stated commitment. |
| `halal food` → a non-halal restaurant | **Not Offensive** | Any restaurant can serve suitable food. Stated in the guideline. |

## The two-way test

Before you commit to Offensive at either level, both of these must be true:

1. **Name the person.** Say out loud who is harmed and how: *a person trying to stop drinking, shown a liquor store*. If you cannot name a person and a harm, it is not offensive.
2. **Name the trigger.** Point at the thing in the **query** that makes this ad a problem. If the only thing you can point at is the ad, you are rating the ad alone, which this task never does.

Before you commit to Not Offensive, one thing must be true:

3. **You checked the query for a hidden trigger.** A query can carry a class-A to class-F trigger without an obvious word. `Great Wolf Lodge` is a family resort. `prenatal` is medical. `Legoland` is for children. If you did not look the place up, you have not checked.

## Common miscalibrations

**Over-rating, by far the most common failure**

- Rating a pair Offensive because it is irrelevant. Explicitly wrong: the guideline says a pair may be odd or surprising and still not offensive.
- Rating any alcohol, casino or adult ad Offensive on sight. Against a generic query it is Not Offensive, and the guideline gives three worked examples of exactly that.
- Rating competitors Offensive. Never.
- Rating a cross-religion pairing Offensive. Explicitly excluded.
- Rating a restaurant Offensive for a vegetarian or halal query. Explicitly excluded, twice.
- Treating Some as a safe middle for anything you are unsure about. Some means a real ethical or tonal clash that a real minority would object to, not your uncertainty.

**Under-rating**

- Missing a family-audience query because it contains no child word. `Legoland` and `Great Wolf Lodge` are class A.
- Missing a medical query that reads as ordinary. `urgent care` is medical, and an alcohol ad against it is Majority.
- Missing a safety context. `gas station` and `driving school` are about to put someone behind a wheel.
- Reading a misspelling literally instead of taking the obvious intent. `preschoool` is a preschool.
- Deciding the ad is fine because the business itself is legal and normal. Every hazard in the matrix is a legal, normal business. The pairing is what is rated.

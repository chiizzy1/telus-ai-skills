# Category Catalog

Use with `../SKILL.md`. The guideline defines seventeen named categories across the three ratings. Naming the category is the step that decides the rating, and the tool asks for it, so never skip to a verdict.

## The three-step procedure

Work these in order and stop at the first yes. Each tier answers a different question.

**Step 1 — Is the expansion the same thing as the keyword, written differently?**
A spelling, spacing, ordering, script, number, length or language transformation of the same term. If yes, it is **Good**. Name which of the nine operations it is.

**Step 2 — Are they different things that serve the same user intent?**
Competitors, or apps sharing a function, or a brand and the function it is known for, or two unlike phrases meaning the same thing. If yes, it is **Acceptable**. Name which of the four.

**Step 3 — Otherwise the intents differ.**
**Bad.** Name which of the four failure types.

The tiers are not degrees of similarity. Good is *the same term*. Acceptable is *different terms, same goal*. Bad is *different goal*. Two apps can look nothing alike and still be Acceptable (`fitness` and `Workout`), and two phrases can share most of their words and still be Bad (`picture editor` and `video editor`).

## Good: nine categories

Rate Good when keyword and expansion look similar to each other.

| # | Category | What it is | Guideline examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Spell correction** | A misspelling that does not make the term unrecognisable, or a correction of one | `utube` → `youtube`, `timu` → `temu` |
| 2 | **Space** | Spaces added or removed without losing recognisability | `tiktok` → `tik tok`, `match.com` → `match com`, `mario kart` → `mariokart` |
| 3 | **Reordering** | Same words, different order, same meaning | `google earth` → `earth google` |
| 4 | **Transliteration** | The same term rendered in another script | `Douyin` → `抖音` |
| 5 | **Singular/Plural** | One side singular, the other plural | `racing game` → `racing games` |
| 6 | **Abbreviations** | Full name against a well-known short form | `instagram` → `ig` |
| 7 | **Same meaning with addition or removal of words** | Words added or dropped without changing intent | `strategy` → `strategy game`, `photo edit` → `photo editor`, `Free games` → `games`, `running apps` → `running apps with GPS`, `google maps` → `google`, `mcdonalds` → `mcdonalds austria` |
| 8 | **Former app name** | One side is the app's former name | `Twitter` → `X` |
| 9 | **Translation** | The two are translations of each other | `Banking` → `Bankwesen`, `Cash transfer` → `Überweisung` |

Category 7 is the broadest and the one most often needed. It covers a qualifier added (`strategy game`), a qualifier removed (`Free games` → `games`), a grammatical form change (`photo edit` → `photo editor`), a locale added (`mcdonalds austria`), and a brand narrowed to its parent (`google maps` → `google`).

## Acceptable: four categories

Rate Acceptable when, despite visual differences, the two share a relationship and an intent.

| # | Category | What it is | Guideline examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Competitors** | Two brands competing for the same users | `adidas` → `Nike`, `linkedin` → `indeed`, `disney` → `hulu`, `door dash` → `kfc`, `UNO` → `Monopoly`, `Amazon` → `Shein`, `Character ai` → `Chatgpt` |
| 2 | **Brand terms, not directly competing, same functionality or features** | Two brands that overlap on a function without being rivals | `capcut` → `TikTok`, `uber` → `instacart`, `discord` → `viber`, `amazon` → `sams club` |
| 3 | **Brand term offering the same functionality as a non-brand term, or the reverse** | A brand against the function it is known for | `Echo` → `Alexa`, `instagram` → `Reels`, `Nike` → `running app`, `Adobe` → `Photo editor`, `scanner pro` → `scanner app`, `whatsapp` → `Free calls`, `multiplayer` → `fortnite` |
| 4 | **Non-brand terms that look different but mean the same** | Two ordinary phrases with one goal | `fitness` → `running`, `fitness` → `Workout`, `games` → `puzzles`, `graphic design` → `logo maker`, `diet tracker` → `calorie counter`, `collage maker` → `picture editor` |

## Bad: four categories

Rate Bad when the keyword does not share the intent of the expansion.

| # | Category | What it is | Guideline examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Different intent, sometimes same genre** | Two brands from unrelated industries, or from the same industry without a shared intent. Also a generic brand keyword expanding to a specific expansion with misaligned intent | `Coinbase` → `Amazon`, `chanel` → `home depot`, `american express` → `coinbase`, `disneyland` → `hulu`, `App` → `Netflix`, `Free` → `Facebook` |
| 2 | **Brand term offering different functionality than a non-brand term** | The brand does not do the thing the other side names | `khan academy` → `brain games`, `games` → `shein` |
| 3 | **Brand terms with different functionality or features** | Two brands whose functions do not overlap, including two products of one company | `google maps` → `google translator`, `kindle` → `alexa`, `addidas` → `espn`, `duolingo` → `Tiktok`, `Amazon fresh grocery` → `Amazon Alexa` |
| 4 | **Non-brand terms that look different and mean something different** | Two ordinary phrases with different goals | `picture editor` → `video editor`, `keto` → `cookbook`, `cooking games` → `shooting games`, `Adventure games` → `Horror games` |

## Choosing between adjacent categories

**Good 7 or Acceptable 4?** Ask whether the expansion is the *same term with words changed* or a *different term with the same goal*. `shooting games` → `online shooting games` is the same term plus a word, so Good 7. `collage maker` → `picture editor` is two different phrases that happen to mean the same, so Acceptable 4.

**Acceptable 1 or Acceptable 3?** Competitors are two brands. Acceptable 3 always has a non-brand side. `Nike` → `running app` is 3. `adidas` → `Nike` is 1. The rating is the same either way, so do not agonise; name the closer one.

**Acceptable 3 or Bad 2?** Both pair a brand with a function. The question is purely factual: does that brand actually offer that function? The guideline makes the point explicitly at `Nike` → `running app`, which is Acceptable because Nike is known for a running app, and adds that the same pair "would be rated Bad for other sports brands like adidas, since they don't have running apps". **Research settles this, not intuition.**

**Acceptable 4 or Bad 4?** Same shape, opposite finding. Both sides are ordinary phrases; the question is whether the goal is one goal. `diet tracker` → `calorie counter` is one goal. `keto` → `cookbook` is not, because one is a specific diet and the other is a generic term.

**Bad 1 or Bad 3?** Bad 1 is different intent, Bad 3 is different functionality, and they overlap heavily. Both are Bad, so pick the nearer description and move on.
